# Open and use an app

You installed a LabConstrictor app ([how to install](../install/index.md)). Here is what to do next.

## Open it

- **Windows:** from the Start Menu or your desktop.
- **macOS:** from the Applications folder.
- **Linux:** from your applications menu.

Jupyter Lab opens in your default web browser, and the **Welcome notebook** appears. It lists the notebooks the app contains. Pick one and follow the instructions written in it. The [notebook usage guide](../notebook-usage/index.md) walks through this with pictures.

## Use it without installing

Many apps also run in Google Colab. The app's page in the [App Centre](../../apps/index.md) lists its Colab notebooks and, when it has one, a pip command.

## Find the app's own documentation

Every app page has a **Using it** section. It links to the app's documentation, or to its README on GitHub when the authors have not written a separate one. What an app does, and what its notebooks expect, is described there, not on this site.

## Something went wrong

- Check the [install guide](../install/index.md): Windows and macOS warn about installers that are not code-signed yet, and both have a way to continue.
- The [troubleshooting guide](../troubleshooting-installed-app/index.md) covers known problems, such as an app that still shows in Windows' list after uninstalling and a certificate error that stops JupyterLab starting.
- Open an issue in the app's repository (linked as **Report a problem** on its page).
