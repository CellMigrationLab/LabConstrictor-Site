# content/how

- `how.js`, `how.css`: the stepper of the How it works page (copied to `docs/assets/how/` by `scripts/lcsite/pages_how.py`).
- `figures/step-N.webp`: one picture per step, **crops of Figure 2 of the LabConstrictor preprint** (Hidalgo-Cenalmor et al., 2026,
  [doi:10.48550/arXiv.2603.10704](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.2603.10704)), which is licensed **CC BY 4.0**. They are made by
  `tools/how_figures/crop_preprint_figure.py` (the boxes are listed there) and must not be edited by hand. The numbered circles and titles
  of the paper are left out because the site numbers its steps differently. The page credits the figure under every picture.
