An illustration: the real card is built by the site from the real file. At least one way to get the app is required, so the last one cannot be switched off.

Further fields add what the app works with and produces, its limitations, status, screenshots and the paper to cite. The site shows only what the file says, and says "not stated" or leaves out what it does not. A workflow checks the file whenever it changes; it never blocks building installers.
